LoanX is a loan management software. Currently, It is only available for android, but will be available for other platform very soon. It can be used by a lender and a borrower, both.

Problem:- 
In the different part of the world, Since Ancient times, loans has been given to the borrower in the exchange of valuable items, often termed as mortgage.
This type of loan is called as mortgage loan. And the facilitator of this type of loan is termed as Pawnshop.
In India, this type of lending is prominently done by the jewellery shops, which kept the jewellery items as mortgage.
But this type of practice is not done only in India but also in other countries like USA, UK, France, Germany, etc.
Not only the jewellery shops but there are many other lenders who keep the valuable items as mortgage.

Traditionally, the lender has to keep the record of the loan in a book, which is called as mortgage book.
This is a very time consuming and tedious task.

Whenever the borrower comes for taking the mortgage and paying the loan, the lender has to search the book when it was taken by the borrower.
Sometimes, it also happens that borrower has taken the loan from different shop but the leander keeps searching the book. And at last, didn;t find anything.


LoanX is a revolutionary app to keep records of loans provided by the unorganized sector in the wotrld without any paperwork.
Every loan is recorded in the app and can be viewed by the borrower and the lender.
